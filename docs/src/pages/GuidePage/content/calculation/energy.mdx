In building Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), operational energy consumption (electricity, space heating, and gas) is evaluated under **Life Cycle Module B6 (Operational Energy Use)**.

Because national electricity grids and district heating networks decarbonize over time, energy emission factors are not static constants. Instead, standards define time-series projections with milestone emission factors for future years.

LCAx provides built-in helper functions (`get_energy_assemblies` in Python/Rust and `getEnergyAssemblies` in TypeScript) to generate standard-compliant default energy assemblies.

## Supported Standards

### Danish Building Regulations (BR18)

When calling `get_energy_assemblies("BR18")`, LCAx generates three discrete energy carrier assemblies:

| Carrier | Assembly ID | Classification Code | Classification Name | Unit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grid Electricity** | `br18-electricity` | `El` | Operational Electricity | `kWh` |
| **District Heating** | `br18-district-heating` | `Fjernvarme` | Operational Heating | `kWh` |
| **Natural Gas** | `br18-natural-gas` | `Ledningsgas` | Operational Gas | `kWh` |

Each assembly contains:
- Standard classification tagging with `system: "BR18"`.
- Unit set to `kWh` and default initial quantity `1.0`.
- A single product representing the carrier with a reference service life of 50 years.
- Five generic impact data milestones for years **2023**, **2025**, **2030**, **2035**, and **2040**, reflecting the official Danish energy projection data.

## Calculation Mechanics

### Milestone Time-Series Interpolation

When calculating impacts for module `B6`:
1. LCAx inspects the milestone data points attached to the product.
2. It determines the evaluation time window from the project start year (by default, the first milestone year, e.g. 2023) to `startYear + referenceStudyPeriod` (e.g. 50 years).
3. Emission factors between consecutive milestone years are linearly interpolated. Beyond the final milestone (e.g. after 2040), the last known emission factor is held constant.
4. The integral over the study period is divided by the study period length to compute a single **time-weighted average emission factor** (e.g. `kg CO2e / kWh`).

### Customizing the Start Year

If your building project starts in a different year (for example, completion in 2025 instead of 2023), set `startYear` on the product's `meta_data`:

- **Python**: `electricity.products[0].meta_data["startYear"] = 2025`
- **TypeScript**: `electricity.products[0].metaData = { ...electricity.products[0].metaData, startYear: 2025 }`
- **Rust**: `product.meta_data.get_or_insert_default().insert("startYear".into(), Some(AnyValue::Number(2025.into())));`

LCAx will evaluate the time-weighted average starting from that specific year.

### Lifetime vs. Annual Quantities (`isAnnual`)

Operational energy inputs can be expressed either as total energy consumed over the building's reference study period (lifetime total) or as an annual consumption rate:

- **Lifetime Demand (`isAnnual: false`)**: By default, BR18 energy assemblies created by LCAx have `isAnnual: false` in their metadata. The product's `quantity` is interpreted as the **total kWh over the lifetime**. LCAx multiplies the total quantity directly by the time-weighted average emission factor without multiplying by the study period again.
- **Annual Consumption Rate (`isAnnual: true`)**: If your energy model provides annual consumption (e.g. `1,000 kWh/year`), set `isAnnual` to `true` in `meta_data`. LCAx will automatically multiply the annual quantity by the reference study period (e.g. 50 years) when computing total impacts.
